## 📖 Overview

EcoTrack is an interactive carbon footprint awareness platform designed to encourage sustainable living. Users can calculate their annual carbon emissions, track daily eco-friendly actions, participate in challenges, earn rewards, and learn practical ways to reduce their environmental impact.

The platform transforms sustainability into an engaging experience through gamification, progress tracking, and personalized insights.
